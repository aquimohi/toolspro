TEMPORAL ECHO
Category: Time Manipulation

DESCRIPTION:
The ability to create temporal echoes of oneself that exist 3-5 seconds in the past or future. These echoes can perform actions independently but are semi-transparent and cannot physically interact with solid matter. The user can see through their echoes' eyes and coordinate complex maneuvers.

VISUAL EFFECTS:
Shimmering, translucent copies of the user with a blue-white glow. Leaves trailing afterimages when moving.

ACTIVATION:
Requires intense focus and a specific hand gesture. User must maintain concentration to keep echoes active.

POWER LEVELS:
Beginner:
Create one echo for up to 10 seconds

Intermediate:
Create up to three echoes simultaneously

Master:
Create five echoes and extend duration to 30 seconds

STRENGTHS & APPLICATIONS:
- Reconnaissance without risk
- Confuse enemies in combat
- Solve temporal puzzles

LIMITATIONS & DRAWBACKS:
- Causes severe headaches with prolonged use
- Cannot create echoes in complete darkness
- Echoes vanish if user loses consciousness

EVOLUTION POTENTIAL:
Could eventually create echoes that can briefly interact with physical objects or even split consciousness between multiple timelines.

SYNERGIES:
- Enhanced speed
- Illusion powers

WEAKNESS/COUNTER:
Temporal dampening fields or abilities that anchor targets to a single timeline

PSYCHOLOGICAL IMPACT:
Users often experience mild dissociation and may struggle with linear thinking. Some develop anxiety about potential futures.

ORIGIN:
Gained after surviving a near-death experience during a temporal anomaly or experiment with experimental time-dilation technology.
