The celebrations

Five days, one story

Every ritual, in the order we lived it

01
Haldi

Turmeric & morning light

Yellow paste, marigold petals, and every aunt with an opinion on how much to apply. The morning the color of the whole week is set.

02
Mehndi

Henna, hands, and hours of gossip

Cones of henna, a courtyard of cousins, and a design that takes longer to dry than anyone has patience for.

03
Sangeet

The night both families forgot the choreography

Dance rehearsals that fell apart on cue, a DJ who read the room perfectly, and a dance floor that never really emptied.

04
Baraat & Phere

Seven circles, one promise each

The groom's procession, the mandap, the fire as witness — and seven vows walked, one slow circle at a time.

05
Reception

The first toast as a married couple

Fairy lights, a first dance neither of us rehearsed enough, and the quiet after everyone finally sat down to eat.
